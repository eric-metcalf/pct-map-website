# Welcome to Dori's PCT Travel Journal

Join me on an incredible journey along the **Pacific Crest Trail** - a 2,650-mile adventure from Mexico to Canada through California, Oregon, and Washington.

## About the Journey

The Pacific Crest Trail (PCT) is one of the most iconic long-distance hiking trails in the world. This journal documents the daily experiences, challenges, and breathtaking moments encountered along the way.
